Let's get you on the water

Ready, set, paddle.

Complete the steps in order. Each step unlocks the next one.

Step 01 · Required first

Sign your liability waiver.

Before choosing equipment or booking, every person joining the trip must complete the official Adventure Capital waiver. For minors, a parent or legal guardian must complete the applicable consent.

Open official liability waiver ↗

Your actual signed waiver must be validated by the operator. This website does not capture signatures.

Step 02 · Find your ride

Choose your watercraft.

Select the type of boat you'd like. Final inventory, reservation times, and prices are confirmed during Peek checkout.

Published base prices from FloVibez; additional taxes/fees or timing restrictions may apply.

Step 03 · Know before you go

Learn your route. Paddle safely.

Review the boundaries and emergency rules before purchasing your rental. The illustration below is not a navigational map; follow signs and on-site instructions.

↗ Upstream: turn around at the first suspension bridge (approximately 5 minutes from launch).
↘ Downstream: turn around at the “Now Leaving HRSP” sign (approximately 60–70 minutes downstream). Travel time varies with conditions.
🦺 Wear your PFDKeep a properly fitted life jacket on at all times.
⛈ Weather firstDo not launch in lightning, heavy rain, or unsafe wind. Follow all closure notices.
↩ Return on timeAllow plenty of time to paddle back and secure equipment before the required return time.
📱 In an emergencyCall 911. For rental assistance, use the official FloVibez contact information or find an authorized park attendant.
⚠️ Never approach the rapids, cross marked rental boundaries, or paddle beyond posted signs. If your craft capsizes, stay with it when safe, keep your life jacket on, and seek assistance.
Step 04 · Secure checkout

Reserve and pay.

You're ready to book. The secure booking process opens through our existing Peek reservation system. Choose the correct watercraft and available time before paying.

Step 05 · Almost on the water

Collect your gear. Enjoy the river.

Your final step is to get your equipment released through the authorized process. A booking confirmation is not an unlock code.

🔒 Equipment release requires authorization.
Have your waiver and payment confirmation ready. Follow the onsite instructions or contact the concession team for verified unlock and equipment release. Never force a lock or remove unattended equipment.
1 · Fit your life jacketFasten and adjust it before entering your boat.
2 · Check the boatInspect the hull, paddle and seating. Report any damage.
3 · Stay inside the limitsFollow the river boundaries and turn around early.
4 · Return your equipmentSecure the boat, paddle and life jacket according to onsite return instructions.
↗ Visit FloVibez for assistance

Call 911 for an emergency. If the concession is closed, do not assume staff are available: launch only when the authorized self-service release process is operational and park rules permit it.
