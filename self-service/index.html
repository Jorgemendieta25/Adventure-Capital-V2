Step 03 · Know before you go

Learn your route. Paddle safely.

Review the boundaries and emergency rules before purchasing your rental. Before you paddle, get familiar with the permitted route. This simplified orientation graphic is not a navigational map; always follow signs and on-site instructions.

↗ Upstream: turn around at the first suspension bridge (approximately 5 minutes from launch).
↘ Downstream: turn around at the “Now Leaving HRSP” sign (approximately 60–70 minutes downstream). Travel time varies with conditions.
🦺 Wear your PFDKeep a properly fitted life jacket on at all times.
⛈ Weather firstDo not launch in lightning, heavy rain, or unsafe wind. Follow all closure notices.
↩ Return on timeAllow plenty of time to paddle back and secure equipment before the required return time.
📱 In an emergencyCall 911. For rental assistance, use the official FloVibez contact information or find an authorized park attendant.
⚠️ Never approach the rapids, cross marked rental boundaries, or paddle beyond posted signs. If your craft capsizes, stay with it when safe, keep your life jacket on, and seek assistance.
